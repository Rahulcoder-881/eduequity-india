---
name: Education source freshness
description: Durable source-selection guidance for EduEquity India education statistics and external references.
---

Use UDISE+ 2024–25 reports/dashboard for school infrastructure, enrollment, and dropout figures, and use PIB or the Union Budget portal for current budget announcements. Treat ASER 2024 and NFHS-5 as the latest comparable survey sources unless a newer official release is verified.

**Why:** Government and NGO sites frequently move deep PDF and donation paths; stale links and stale year labels can remain valid-looking while returning 404s or old figures.

**How to apply:** When refreshing content, verify the source page first, prefer canonical landing pages when deep links are unstable, and label values with the exact data year rather than the current calendar year.
